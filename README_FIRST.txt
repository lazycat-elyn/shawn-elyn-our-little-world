MASTER 3.2 — SUPERMARKET LIFE GAME 1.0

THIS VERSION REPLACES THE OLD "BUY ONE GROCERY BOX" SUPERMARKET WITH AN ACTUAL PLAYABLE LOCATION.

PLAYABLE FLOW
Go Out -> Supermarket
or
Drive Lexus -> park -> Supermarket

1. Enter a real walkable supermarket scene
2. Walk with WASD / Arrow Keys or tap/click the floor
3. Take a trolley from the entrance
4. Shopping List is automatically built from low home inventory
5. Click a Shopping List item to walk toward the correct aisle
6. Browse physical aisles: Produce, Dairy, Meat/Seafood, Pantry, Snacks/Drinks, Frozen, Pet/Household
7. Pick individual products from shelves
8. Products visibly appear in trolley
9. 57 distinct product art assets are included; product images are not reused under different names
10. Freshness, stock, LAST PACK, sold out and sale discount labels
11. Shawn/Elyn companion can trigger a small "secret chips" event
12. Checkout queue
13. Scanner timing mini-game for each product
14. Produce weighing mini-game
15. Bagging mini-game: keep Bag A/B/C under 4.5kg
16. Full receipt, subtotal, discount, checkout score and Coins payment
17. If you drove there: physically load bags into Lexus boot
18. Drive the normal City Drive route back home
19. Park at home
20. Open boot -> carry bags -> sort Fridge / Pantry / Pet & Household Supplies
21. Home inventory only increases after items are put away

MOBILE
- Tap-to-move
- Large directional pad
- Large context Action button
- Bottom-sheet shelf browser
- Checkout and bagging reflow for small screens
- No requirement to play the supermarket in landscape

PRESERVED
- MASTER 2.6.2 clean character mode
- City Drive
- Super Racing 2.0
- Fishing
- Kitchen / Recipes
- Fridge
- Go Out
- Pets / Tasks / Economy
