MASTER 2.8 — CINEMATIC CAR GAMEPLAY UI

This patch changes the actual Car Gameplay driving screen to follow the approved reference image:
- scenic lakeside/city/café road instead of the flat polygon road
- pastel white rounded HUD panels
- left ETA/speed/follow distance/traffic/weather
- right destination preview + map
- steering wheel + Brake/Accel pedals
- camera tabs
- couple card
- live Driving Score
- Parking Challenge Next card

Existing Car 2.0 physics, rules, traffic events, difficulty modes and Parking 2.0 remain.
MASTER 2.6.2 clean-character mode remains in app.js.
