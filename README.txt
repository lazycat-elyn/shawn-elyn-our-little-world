- multiple cameras: third-person, first-person, reverse camera
- controls: accelerate, brake, steer, D/R, handbrake, indicators, horn, headlights, wipers
- real road rules/events: red light, pedestrian, speed camera, speed bump, slow car, roadwork, merge, turns, rain
- rain grip, night/headlights, rain/wiper visibility
- traffic collisions, road shoulder, fuel, condition, cleanliness
- Shawn/Elyn passenger reactions: phone, drink, sleep, sudden braking, parking comments
- separate parking game after route: EASY/NORMAL/HARD/SPECIAL parallel
- parking checks position, angle, collision, curb
- driving score combines safety, smoothness, rules, signals and parking
- interactive exit sequence: engine off -> unbuckle -> open door -> get out
- boot/back seat/glove box/cup holder storage
- supermarket purchases first go into the boot; unload at home/garage
- multi-step refuel interaction
- multi-step 7-stage car wash
